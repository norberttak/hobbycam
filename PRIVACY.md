# HobbyCAM privacy policy

HobbyCAM collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising.

The program does not use the network. The only exception is **Help → User manual**, which asks your web browser to open
the online manual (<https://norberttak.github.io/hobbycam/>); from then on the browser's and GitHub Pages' own policies apply.

Your drawings, projects, G-code files and tool library stay on your computer, in the places you choose and in the settings
folder described in the manual. Nothing is sent to the author or to anyone else.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/norberttak/hobbycam/issues>.
